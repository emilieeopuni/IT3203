Research References

  • Mockapetris, P. (1983). RFC 882: Domain Names—Concepts and Facilities . The original DNS proposal.
  • Mockapetris, P. (1987). RFC 1034: Domain Names—Concepts and Facilities . Explains DNS organization and name resolution.
  • Mockapetris, P. (1987). RFC 1035: Domain Names—Implementation and Specification . Describes DNS records and protocol details.
  • Arends, R., et al. (2005). RFC 4033: DNS Security Introduction and Requirements . Introduces DNSSEC.
  • Hoffman, P., and McManus, P. (2018). RFC 8484: DNS Queries over HTTPS . Explains DNS over HTTPS.
  • Hu, Z., et al. (2016). RFC 7858: DNS over Transport Layer Security . Explains DNS over TLS.
